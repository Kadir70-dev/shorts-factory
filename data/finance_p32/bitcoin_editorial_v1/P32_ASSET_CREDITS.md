## Commercial-use compatibility

- **Public domain and CC0:** commercial use allowed, attribution not required (credited anyway below as good practice).
- **CC BY 2.0 / 4.0:** commercial use allowed. Requires attribution, a licence link, and an indication that the work was changed.
- **CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0:** commercial use allowed. Requires attribution, a licence link and a change notice. The adapted plates must also be offered under the same CC BY-SA licence. Most guidance treats a video that merely includes the plate as a collection, but **legal review should confirm this**.
- **Other risks to clear:** currency reproduction (s126), personality rights for identifiable living people (s82, s96), and the weak PD basis of s56.
