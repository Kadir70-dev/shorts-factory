## Typography plates (no third-party images)

- **s88 (DigiCash), s111 (Liberty Dollar), s126 (Z$100 trillion):** original K70 typography on a generated texture, set in Noto Serif/Sans (SIL Open Font License).
- **s111 date verification:** *United States v. von NotHaus*, W.D.N.C. No. 5:09CR27-RLV, order of 1 Dec 2014. The order states that the search of the Liberty Dollar HQ in Evansville occurred on November 14, 2007, and that the Sunshine Minting search ran 14–15 Nov 2007. Source: https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/united-states-v-von-888560842.
- **s126:** the banknote image was withdrawn because Commons flags "Files that may be protected by currency protection laws" and the Zimbabwean reproduction rules could not be verified from an authoritative source. No banknote is reproduced.
