## Verification notes

- **s15:** PD-scan of a Reichsbank note (PD-Germany-§134-KUG; PD-1996 in the US); Commons carries no currency-restriction tag. The Papiermark has been demonetized for about 100 years, and §128 OWiG only covers images confusable "im Zahlungsverkehr" (in payment). Low risk; not legal advice.
- **s16:** a photograph of a 1923 medal. The photographer released it (`self`), and the medal itself is PD (PD-EU-no author disclosure; PD-1996).
- **s25:** JEC hearings "Employment, Growth, and Price Levels", 26–30 Oct 1959, Part 9A (Triffin, p. 2905), per FRASER / St. Louis Fed.
- **s111:** *U.S. v. von NotHaus*, W.D.N.C. 5:09CR27-RLV, order of 1 Dec 2014: Evansville HQ searched 14 Nov 2007.
- **s117:** Decree 1570/2001, signed 1 Dec 2001, published 3 Dec 2001 (Marval O'Farrell Mairal).
